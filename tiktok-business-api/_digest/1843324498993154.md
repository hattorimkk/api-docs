# Create Upgraded Smart+ Lead Generation Campaigns
> Đường dẫn: Use Cases > Campaign creation > Create an Upgraded Smart+ Campaign > Create Upgraded Smart+ Lead Generation Campaigns
> Độ dài nguồn: 2338 ký tự (đã rút gọn)

## Phần mở đầu của nguồn
This article walks you through the steps to create Upgraded Smart+ Lead Generation Campaigns.

Based on your preference for whether to use a catalog and optimization location, you can create an Upgraded Smart+ Lead Generation Campaign with optimization location as Instant Form, Upgraded Smart+ Lead Generation Campaign with optimization location as Website, Upgraded Smart+ Lead Generation Campaign with optimization location as TikTok direct messages, Upgraded Smart+ Lead Generation Campaign with optimization location as instant messaging apps, Upgraded Smart+ Lead Generation Campaign with optimization location as phone call, Upgraded Smart+ Automotive Ads, or Upgraded Smart+ Lead Generic Catalog Ads.

- To create an Upgraded Smart+ Lead Generation Campaign without using a catalog and with optimization location as Instant Form, see [Create an Upgraded Smart+ Lead Generation Campaign with Location as Instant Form](doc:1843324592169986).

- To create an Upgraded Smart+ Lead Generation Campaign without using a catalog and with optimization location as Website, see [Create an Upgraded Smart+ Lead Generation Campaign with Location as Website](doc:1843324606213121).

- To create an Upgraded Smart+ Lead Generation Campaign without using a catalog and with optimization location as TikTok direct messages, see [Create an Upgraded Smart+ Lead Generation Campaign with optimization location as TikTok direct messages](doc:1847302969710913).
