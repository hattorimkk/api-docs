# (To-be-deprecated) Create Video Shopping Ads with products from TikTok Commerce
> Đường dẫn: Use Cases > Campaign creation > Create Shopping Ads > Create Video Shopping Ads > (To-be-deprecated) Create Video Shopping Ads with products from TikTok Commerce
> Độ dài nguồn: 24196 ký tự (đã rút gọn)

## Phần mở đầu của nguồn
TikTok Commerce (formerly known as TikTok Shopping) is a suite of solutions, features, and advertising tools that empowers brands and merchants to meaningfully engage with their customers. TikTok Commerce supports a variety of eCommerce content that lets you sell your products in TikTok videos and on your profile page's product showcase tab.

> **Important:** 
> Starting Q3, 2025, [GMV Max](doc:1822009058467842) will be the default and only supported campaign type for TikTok Shop Ads. If you create ads using the Product Sales objective and TikTok Shop as your sales destination, you will no longer be able to use the API to create, edit, or duplicate [LIVE Shopping Ads](doc:1754162402455554), [Product Shopping Ads](doc:1785886237030401), or [Video Shopping Ads](doc:1741942291730434). Please migrate to the [GMV Max API](doc:1822000911166465) as early as possible. If you have questions or need support, please contact your TikTok representative.  Learn more about [the detailed changes by ad type and endpoint](doc:1837161048383489).

This article introduces how to create Video Shopping Ads with products from TikTok Commerce.
