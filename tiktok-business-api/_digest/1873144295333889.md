# Create Upgraded Smart+ Search Ads Campaigns
> Đường dẫn: Use Cases > Campaign creation > Create an Upgraded Smart+ Campaign > Create Upgraded Smart+ Search Ads Campaigns
> Độ dài nguồn: 978 ký tự (đã rút gọn)

## Phần mở đầu của nguồn
This article guides you through the steps to create Upgraded Smart+ Search Ads Campaigns.

A Search Ads Campaign is a TikTok Ad Campaign that uses keywords to help you reach users searching for specific terms related to your product or service. With these campaigns, you have full control over ads targeted exclusively to the TikTok Search Results Page.

Based on your advertising objective, you can create one of the following campaign types:

- To get more people to install and take desired actions in your app, see [Create an Upgraded Smart+ App Promotion Search Ads Campaign](slug:create-an-upgraded-smart-app-promotion-search-ads-campaign).
- To collect leads for your business, see [Create an Upgraded Smart+ Lead Generation Search Ads Campaign](slug:create-an-upgraded-smart-lead-generation-search-ads-campaign).
- To drive sales on your website or app, see [Create an Upgraded Smart+ Sales Search Ads Campaign](slug:create-an-upgraded-smart-sales-search-ads-campaign).
