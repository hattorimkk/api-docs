# (Deprecated) Create Dynamic Showcase Ads
> Đường dẫn: Use Cases > Campaign creation > (Deprecated) Create Dynamic Showcase Ads
> Độ dài nguồn: 814 ký tự (đã rút gọn)

## Phần mở đầu của nguồn
Dynamic Showcase Ads (DSA, formally known as Dynamic Product Ads or DPA) is an ad solution that dynamically shows ads to people based on how they interacted with your app or website. 

DSA supports TikTok and Pangle placements.

> **Note:** Starting from Feb.16, 2023, you can no longer create Dynamic Showcase Ads.  To ensure a smooth API integration, we recommend you migrate to [Video Shopping Ads](doc:1741942291730434)  under Product Sales objective. The modification of existing Dynamic Showcase Ads through API will be supported till the end of March, and may be extended on a case-by-case basis. If you need extra transition time, please contact your TikTok representatives.

> **Note:** Dynamic Showcase Ads is currently in Beta. If you would like to access it, please contact your TikTok representative.
