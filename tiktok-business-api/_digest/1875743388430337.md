# Create an Upgraded Smart+ Travel Search Ads Campaign for App
> Đường dẫn: Use Cases > Campaign creation > Create an Upgraded Smart+ Campaign > Create Upgraded Smart+ Search Ads Campaigns > Create an Upgraded Smart+ Sales Search Ads Campaign > Create an Upgraded Smart+ Catalog Search Ads Campaign for App > Create an Upgraded Smart+ Travel Search Ads Campaign for App
> Độ dài nguồn: 77189 ký tự (đã rút gọn)

## Phần mở đầu của nguồn
This article explains how to create an Upgraded Smart+ Search Ads Campaign for App using the Sales objective, the app sales destination, and a travel (hotel, flight, or destination) catalog.

This campaign type dynamically leverages your travel catalog products to populate and deliver targeted app ads.

To configure this campaign type, set `sales_destination` to `APP` and `catalog_type` to `TRAVEL_ENTERTAINMENT` at the campaign level. Additionally, specify the hotel, flight, or destination catalog by passing the `catalog_id` at the ad group level. By leveraging Search Ads Campaigns for App, you can effectively grow your user base through two primary strategies: acquiring new users (Prospecting) and re-engaging your existing audience (Retargeting).

Depending on your requirements for [Dedicated Campaigns](slug:dedicated-campaign) (DC), you can create either a non-DC or a DC Search Ads Campaign for App.  For detailed instructions on creating each type, refer to their respective sections: "[Create an Upgraded Smart+ Travel non-DC Search Ads Campaign for App](#item-link-anchor-2)", "[Create an Upgraded Smart+ Travel DC Search Ads Campaign for App](#item-link-anchor-3)".
