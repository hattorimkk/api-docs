## Phần mở đầu của nguồn
This article walks you through the steps to create Upgraded Smart+ TikTok Growth Max campaigns using the Native growth objective.

TikTok Growth Max is a performance advertising solution designed to help advertisers scale content and accelerate end users from discovery to conversion, all within TikTok ecosystem.
Powering the next wave of entertainment growth, TikTok Growth Max is turning immersive gaming and short drama experiences into scalable, incremental business outcomes through frictionless user journeys, rich onsite signals, and automation-powered optimization.

Based on your preference for whether to promote a TikTok Mini Game, Mini Drama, or Drama Series, you can create an Upgraded Smart+ TikTok Growth Max: Mini Games campaign, an Upgraded Smart+ TikTok Growth Max: Mini Dramas campaign, or an Upgraded Smart+ TikTok Growth Max: Drama Series campaign. For detailed instructions on creating each type, refer to their respective articles:
- [Create an Upgraded Smart+ TikTok Growth Max: Mini Games campaign](slug:create-an-upgraded-smart-mini-games-campaign)
- [Create an Upgraded Smart+ TikTok Growth Max: Mini Dramas campaign](slug:create-an-upgraded-smart-minis-campaign)
