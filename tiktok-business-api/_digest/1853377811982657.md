## Phần mở đầu của nguồn
This article walks you through the steps to create an Upgraded Smart+ TikTok Growth Max: Mini Dramas campaign.

Based on your preference for using a catalog to promote TikTok Mini Dramas, you can create Upgraded Smart+ TikTok Growth Max: Mini Dramas Non-Catalog campaigns or Upgraded Smart+ TikTok Growth Max: Mini Dramas Catalog campaigns. For detailed instructions on creating each type, refer to their respective sections: "Create an Upgraded Smart+ TikTok Growth Max: Mini Dramas Non-Catalog campaign" and "Create an Upgraded Smart+ TikTok Growth Max: Mini Dramas Catalog campaign".

# Create an Upgraded Smart+ TikTok Growth Max: Mini Dramas Non-Catalog campaign
An Upgraded Smart+ TikTok Growth Max: Mini Dramas Non-Catalog campaign is an Upgraded Smart+ Campaign promoting a TikTok Mini Dramas without using a mini series catalog.
