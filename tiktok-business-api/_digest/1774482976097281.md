# Create a Lead Generation ad with optimization location as Website
> Đường dẫn: Use Cases > Campaign creation > Create Lead Generation ads > Create a Lead Generation ad with optimization location as Website
> Độ dài nguồn: 17267 ký tự (đã rút gọn)

## Phần mở đầu của nguồn
This article introduces how to create a Lead Generation ad with optimization location as Website, driving lead-related actions on your website.

Based on your preference for using a catalog to promote car inventory or models, you have the flexibility to create three types of Lead Generation ads:
- Standard Lead Generation ads: This type of Lead Generation ad doesn't use a catalog to promote car inventory. For detailed instructions on creating such ads, see Create a Standard Lead Generation ad with optimization location as Website.
- Automotive Ads for Inventory: This type of Lead Generation ad uses a catalog to promote car inventory. For detailed instructions on creating such ads, see [Create Automotive Ads for Inventory](doc:1827752507854850).
- Automotive Ads for Models: This type of Lead Generation ad uses a catalog to promote car models. For detailed instructions on creating such ads, see [Create Automotive Ads for Models](doc:1829635758164994).
