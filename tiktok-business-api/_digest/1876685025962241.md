# Create an Upgraded Smart+ Lead Generation Campaign with multiple location selection
> Đường dẫn: Use Cases > Campaign creation > Create an Upgraded Smart+ Campaign > Create Upgraded Smart+ Lead Generation Campaigns > Create an Upgraded Smart+ Lead Generation Campaign with multiple location selection
> Độ dài nguồn: 35370 ký tự (đã rút gọn)

## Phần mở đầu của nguồn
This article walks you through the steps to create an Upgraded Smart+ Lead Generation Campaign with multiple location selection.

Multiple location selection enables you to deliver multiple lead conversion journeys, such as Instant Form and Website, simultaneously within a single Smart+ Lead Generation campaign. Our system automatically directs users to the destination where they’re most likely to convert — based on their behavior and preferences —helping you drive better results, effortlessly.

> **Note:** 
> Creating an Upgraded Smart+ Lead Generation Campaign with multiple location selection is currently an allowlist-only feature. If you would like to access it, please contact your TikTok representative.
