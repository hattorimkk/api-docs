# Create a Lead Generation ad with optimization location as TikTok direct messages
> Đường dẫn: Use Cases > Campaign creation > Create Lead Generation ads > Create a Lead Generation ad with optimization location as TikTok direct messages
> Độ dài nguồn: 23629 ký tự (đã rút gọn)

## Phần mở đầu của nguồn
This article introduces how to create a Lead Generation ad with optimization location as TikTok direct messages. This enables the ad audience to directly message your authorized TikTok account in the TikTok app by clicking the "Send message" call-to-action button within the ad.

> **Note:** 
>  • Creating a Lead Generation ad with TikTok direct messages as the optimization location is generally available for advertisers registered in the following regions:  • APAC: Australia, Bangladesh, Cambodia, Indonesia, Japan, Malaysia, New Zealand, Pakistan, the Philippines, Singapore, South Korea, Sri Lanka, Taiwan, Thailand, and Vietnam. • LATAM: Argentina, Bolivia, Brazil, Chile, Colombia, Costa Rica, the Dominican Republic, Ecuador, Guatemala, Mexico, Panama, Paraguay, Peru, Puerto Rico, and Uruguay. • For advertisers registered in other regions, this feature is currently an allowlist-only feature. If you would like to access it, please contact your TikTok representative. • You can only create **Spark Ads** as Lead Generation ads with optimization location as TikTok direct messages.

# Prerequisite
The owner of the authorized TikTok account has enabled the "Everyone" permission for Direct Messages on the TikTok account, allowing anyone to send direct messages to the TikTok account. To learn how to configure this setting, refer to [How to manage who can send you direct messages](https://support.tiktok.com/en/using-tiktok/messaging-and-notifications/direct-message-settings#7).
