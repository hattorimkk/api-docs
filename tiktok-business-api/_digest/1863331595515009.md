# Create an Upgraded Smart+ TikTok Growth Max: Drama Series campaign
> Đường dẫn: Use Cases > Campaign creation > Create an Upgraded Smart+ Campaign > Create Upgraded Smart+ TikTok Growth Max campaigns > Create an Upgraded Smart+ TikTok Growth Max: Drama Series campaign
> Độ dài nguồn: 27059 ký tự (đã rút gọn)

## Phần mở đầu của nguồn
This article walks you through the steps to create an Upgraded Smart+ TikTok Growth Max: Drama Series campaign.

An Upgraded Smart+ TikTok Growth Max: Drama Series campaign is an Upgraded Smart+ Campaign promoting a TikTok Series without using a catalog. Series offers exclusive and premium video packages produced by creators. Access to Series is available to purchase through the creator's profile and links attached to TikTok videos.
