# Create a Lead Generation ad with optimization location as instant messaging apps
> Đường dẫn: Use Cases > Campaign creation > Create Lead Generation ads > Create a Lead Generation ad with optimization location as instant messaging apps
> Độ dài nguồn: 45849 ký tự (đã rút gọn)

## Phần mở đầu của nguồn
This article introduces how to create a Lead Generation ad with optimization location as instant messaging apps. This enables the ad audience to take two different actions based on their App installation status by clicking the call-to-action button within the ad:
- If the audience has installed the instant messaging app (such as WhatsApp), they can send a message to you (advertiser) on the App.
- If the audience hasn't installed the instant messaging app, they will be directed to view a Custom TikTok Instant Page.

> **Note:** 
>  • Creating a Lead Generation ad with optimization location as instant messaging apps is generally available for advertisers registered in the following APAC regions: Australia, Bangladesh, Cambodia, Indonesia, Japan, Malaysia, New Zealand, Pakistan, the Philippines, Singapore, South Korea, Sri Lanka, Taiwan, Thailand, and Vietnam. • For advertisers registered in other regions, this feature is currently an allowlist-only feature. If you would like to access it, please contact your TikTok representative.
