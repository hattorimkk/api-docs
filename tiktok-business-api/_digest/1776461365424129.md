> Độ dài nguồn: 40177 ký tự (đã rút gọn)

## Phần mở đầu của nguồn
Below are the supported metrics for Smart+ Campaign reports.

## Basic data

| Metric | Type | Description | Detail |
|---|---|---|---|
| spend | string | Total cost | The estimated total amount of money you've spent on your campaign, ad group or ad during its schedule. |
| cpc | string | CPC (Destination) | The average amount of money to a specified destination costs you. |
| cpm | string | CPM | The average amount of money you've spent per 1,000 impressions. |
| impressions | string | Impressions | The number of times your ads were on screen. |
| clicks | string | Clicks (Destination) | The number of clicks from your ads that lead to specified destinations. |
| ctr | string | CTR (Destination) | The percentage of times people click to a specified destination after being shown your ad. |
| conversion | string | Conversions | The number of times your ad achieved an outcome, based on the objective and settings you selected. ⏎ The total count is calculated based on the time each ad impression occurred. |
| cost_per_conversion | string | CPA | The average amount of money you've spent on a conversion. ⏎ The total count is calculated based on the time each ad impression occurred. |
| conversion_rate | string | CVR (Clicks) | The percentage of conversions from all clicks on your ads. The metric will be removed soon. ⏎ The total count is calculated based on the time each ad impression occurred. |
