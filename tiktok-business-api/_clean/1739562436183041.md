# FAQs
> Đường dẫn: Marketing API > Business Center > Guides > FAQs
# BC Administration

Q1:  Can I use the endpoint [/bc/advertiser/create/](doc:1739939020318721) to create an ad account for our brand?
A: Yes. You can use the endpoint without applying for allowlisting if you have been allowlisted for the Business Center API.

Q2: How can I check the type of my ad account?
A: Now the feature specifically designed for this is unavailable, but you can see the type (`advertiser_type`) by using endpoint [/bc/billing_group/get/](doc:1738944324279297).

Q3:  Can I subscribe to my account status change update?
A：No, you currently cannot.  We now support suscriptions through webhooks (endpoint [/subscription/subscribe/](doc:1739092028876801)) to help you get real-time updates for new leads and the review status of your ad (when the ad is rejected).

# BC Payments

Q1: Why does the data I obtained from endpoint [/advertiser/balance/get/](doc:1739939106470913) seem inaccurate after I added money to my account?
A: After you finish the top-up, the endpoint [/advertiser/balance/get/](doc:1739939106470913) needs 30 seconds to synchronize the change.

Q2:   Could you clarify the exchange rate for Business Center?
A: There is no exchange rate for Business Center. The currency for Business Center is set by the advertiser, and now Business Center doesn’t support currency conversion.

Q3:  Which endpoint can I use to check whether a certain ad account(`advertiser_id`) can be topped-up by a Business Center (`bc_id`)?
A: You can use the endpoint [/bc/asset/member/get/](doc:1739659558931458), and set the `asset_id` as `advertiser_id` in the request. If the response is not null, then the ad account (`advertiser_id`)  can be topped-up by the Business Center (`bc_id`).

# BC Assets

Q1: How can I manage Pixel as an asset in Business Center?
A: You need to have admin permission of the ad account.
First log in to the Business Center platform, and then once you have Admin permission of the ad account, you can use the [/bc/pixel/transfer/](doc:1739659520972802) endpoint and pass in Pixel Code(`pixel_code`) to transfer the pixel to Business Center as asset.
