# Upload playables
> Đường dẫn: Marketing API > Creatives > Guides > Creative assets > Upload playables
This guide walks you through the process of uploading playable assets to the TikTok Creative Library and using them in your ad campaigns via the API.

Playables are interactive, HTML5-based creative assets that let users experience a mini-demo or micro-game of an app directly in their TikTok feed. By offering a "try-before-you-buy" experience, playable creatives help pre-qualify your audience and drive high-intent user acquisition.

## Steps
**1. Upload the playable assets.**

Call the [/playable/upload/](slug:upload-a-playable-creative) endpoint to upload your playable assets. Upon upload, the system automatically validates the assets against TikTok's technical specifications.

2. **Check validation and audit status**.

Call the [/playable/validate/](slug:check-the-status-of-a-playable-creative) endpoint to poll the current validation and audit status of your upload. If the initial validation succeeds, the playable asset automatically enter the audit process.

**3. Save to Creative Library.**

Once the status of a playable reaches `VALIDATE_SUCCESS` or `AUDIT_SUCCESS`, call the [/playable/save/](slug:save-a-playable-creative) endpoint to finalize the upload and save the asset to your Creative Library. The response will return a `playable_url`. Save this URL for the next step.

**Note**: While you can save a playable asset at `VALIDATE_SUCCESS`, only playables that have reached the `AUDIT_SUCCESS` status can actually be delivered in active ad campaigns.

**4. Attach to an ad.**

To use the playable in an Upgraded Smart+ Ad, pass the `playable_url` to the `playable_list` field when calling the [/smart_plus/ad/create/](slug:create-an-upgraded-smart-ad) or [/smart_plus/ad/update/](slug:update-an-upgraded-smart-ad) endpoints.
