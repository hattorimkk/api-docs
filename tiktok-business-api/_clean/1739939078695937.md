# BC Payments
> Đường dẫn: API Reference > BC Payments
Business Center is a one-stop business hub for organizations of all sizes to activate marketing activities and reach desired audiences in a collaborative way on TikTok. BC Payments API enables you to process a payment for an ad account, and get the balances, transaction records, or cost records of ad accounts or a Business Center.

This group consists of the following endpoints:

* [Process a payment](doc:1739939095321601)
* [Get the balance and budget of ad accounts](doc:1739939106470913)
* [Get the balance of a BC ](doc:1739939128198145)
* [Get the transaction records of a BC or ad accounts](doc:1792849810925569)
* [Get the transaction records of ad accounts](doc:1739939116353538)
* [Get the transaction records of a BC](doc:1739939140408322)
* [Get the budget change history of an ad account](doc:1830173979548674)
* [Get the cost records of a BC and ad accounts](doc:1829079287639041)
