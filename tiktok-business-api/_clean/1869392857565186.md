# Troubleshoot Skill issues
> Đường dẫn: Skill and MCP management > Get started with Skill management > Troubleshoot Skill issues
This article covers recovery workflows for common issues during the Skill lifecycle.

## Resubmit a rejected Skill
If your Skill is rejected during the initial review, the TikTok team provides specific, actionable feedback explaining the issues. Address the feedback, update your Skill, and resubmit for another evaluation cycle. Resolving all identified issues thoroughly reduces the likelihood of repeated rejections.

**Steps**

1. On the My Agentic Hub page, locate the Skill displaying a **Rejected** status and click **Manage**.

![locate the Skill displaying a Rejected status and click Manage](https://images.ctfassets.net/5jqet0ab9qci/35Zlfya2uYfSpvFRKpd6QR/6d35a6449bd9c4cd9e35cb28f472067d/locate_the_Skill_displaying_a_Rejected_status_and_click_Manage.png)

2. On the Skill detail page, carefully review the rejection reasons provided by the TikTok review team. Each rejection includes specific feedback.

![carefully review the rejection reasons](https://images.ctfassets.net/5jqet0ab9qci/2i2CAJyh6LALi7iwDQR1SU/2ee7042b21a8890028c2281aa4346ce0/carefully_review_the_rejection_reasons.png)

3. Click **Resubmit for Review** to re-open the upload form.

4. Update the relevant fields to address the feedback. All of the following fields are editable:
- **Detailed Description**
- **Version**
- **Skill File Upload**
- **Skill Category**
- **Industry**
- **Showcase prompts**
- **Developer Contact Email**
- **Legal Disclaimer / Privacy Policy**
- **Countries**

> **Important:** 
> The **Skill Name** field is permanent and cannot be modified after the initial upload. If the rejection feedback relates to your Skill name, you must delete the Skill and create a new one with the desired name. See [Upload a new Skill](https://business-api.tiktok.com/portal/docs/upload-and-publish-skills/v1.3#item-link-Upload%20a%20new%20Skill).

![Update the relevant fields to address the feedback](https://images.ctfassets.net/5jqet0ab9qci/3ktRIP7VOnW04s7WQvB2bq/2887a26457852b4e6ba4f9fcff175dbd/Resubmit_a_rejected_Skill_EN_3.png)

5. Review and accept the terms and conditions.

6. Click **Submit Version** to finalize your resubmission.

![Click Submit Version to finalize your resubmission](https://images.ctfassets.net/5jqet0ab9qci/6LgyRQxgDFZoPWfCC2hJD0/6df15bd4cdd8f5357f3fcf8b02496a43/Click_Submit_Version_to_finalize_your_resubmission..png)

**What happens next**

Your Skill re-enters the review queue. Reviews typically complete within **five to seven business days**. Track the status from the **My Agentic Hub** page. If your Skill is approved, you can publish it immediately or defer publication.

## Handle a rejected version update
When you upload a new version of a published Skill and that new version is rejected, your existing published version remains live and unaffected. Advertisers experience no disruption.

**How to identify a rejected version update**
- On the My Agentic Hub page, your Skill continues to display a **Published** status (because the original version is still live).

![your Skill continues to display a Published status](https://images.ctfassets.net/5jqet0ab9qci/4wCxsELIJ7QnFQLGiwvbhR/ef8c588fadcbf177544b65021f2cdd4b/your_Skill_continues_to_display_a_Published_status.png)

- On the Skill detail page, the new version displays a **Rejected** status alongside the reviewer's feedback.

![the new version displays a Rejected status](https://images.ctfassets.net/5jqet0ab9qci/73xFB72sZHAKpKlijiF5ws/7237a1692b64fb1f6ee23fc899c7c959/the_new_version_displays_a_Rejected_status.png)

**Steps to resolve**

1. On the My Agentic Hub page, locate the Skill and click **Manage**.

2. On the Skill detail page, review the rejection feedback for the new version.

3. Click **Resubmit for Review** to re-open the upload form.

![Click Resubmit for Review to re-open the upload form](https://images.ctfassets.net/5jqet0ab9qci/Khmb4nwUHrbeY3eMuKOSR/021b969e4373f6052492b57486b0870a/Click_Resubmit_for_Review_to_re-open_the_upload_form.png)

4. Address the identified issues by updating the relevant fields:
- **Detailed Description**
- **Version**: must remain higher than the currently published version
- **Skill File Upload**
- **Skill Category**
- **Industry**
- **Showcase prompts**
- **Developer Contact Email**
- **Legal Disclaimer / Privacy Policy**
- **Countries**
5. Accept the terms and conditions.

6. Click **Submit Version** to resubmit.

After submission, the new version enters the review queue (typically **five to seven business days**).
