# Verification
> Đường dẫn: API Reference > Verification
When you create a Business Center or ad account on TikTok, you'll be asked to verify your business information. This process ensures a secure ecosystem by authenticating the identities of all advertisers and businesses operating on the platform. Verification API enables you to upload required verification documents and complete either business or personal identity verification for your accounts.

For step-by-step instructions on the verification workflow, see [Verify your Business Center account or ad account on TikTok](slug:verify-your-business-center-account-or-ad-account-on-tiktok).

This group consists of the following endpoints:
- [Get available verification document types for a region](slug:get-available-verification-document-types-for-a-region)
- [Upload verification documents](slug:upload-verification-documents)
- [Submit a verification request for your account](slug:submit-a-verification-request-for-your-account)
- [Check the verification status of your account](slug:check-the-verification-status-of-your-account)
