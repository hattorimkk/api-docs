# Verify your Business Center account or ad account on TikTok
> Đường dẫn: Marketing API > Business Center > Guides > Assets > Assets > Verify your Business Center account or ad account on TikTok
When you create a Business Center or ad account on TikTok, you'll be asked to verify your business information. This process ensures a secure ecosystem by authenticating the identities of all advertisers and businesses operating on the platform.

You can complete your Business Center or ad account verification programmatically via API. Review the workflow below for detailed instructions.

## Steps
**1. Check the current verification status of your business.** 

Call [/account/verification/status/](slug:check-the-verification-status-of-your-account) and pass either `bc_id` or `advertiser_id` to retrieve the account's current `verification_status`. If the returned `verification_status` is `NOT_SUBMITTED`, `FAILED`, or `EXPIRED`, review any provided `rejection_reason` and proceed to step 2.

**2. Check the available verification document types**. 

Call [/account/verification/filetype/](slug:get-available-verification-document-types-for-a-region) to retrieve the list of supported business or individual identity verification document types for your specific country or region. Make sure to note the returned `file_type_code`.

**3. Upload your verification documents**. 

Call [/account/verification/upload/](slug:upload-verification-documents) to upload up to two verification documents. Make sure to note the returned `qualification_image_ids`.

**4. Submit the verification request.**

Call [/account/verification/submit/](slug:submit-a-verification-request-for-your-account) and provide either `business_form` (for business verification) or `individual_form` (for individual identity verification). You need to also include the `file_type_code` from step 2 and the `qualification_image_ids` from step 3.

**5. Check the updated verification status.** 

Call [/account/verification/status/](slug:check-the-verification-status-of-your-account) again with your `bc_id` or `advertiser_id` to check the updated `verification_status`. If the status is still `NOT_SUBMITTED`, `FAILED`, or `EXPIRED`, check the `rejection_reason` and return to step 3 to upload corrected documents.
