# Obtain pre-review results for your creatives
> Đường dẫn: Marketing API > Creatives > Guides > Creative tools > Obtain pre-review results for your creatives
This guide walks you through the process of obtaining pre-review results for your creatives using Creative Pre-review API.

# Introduction
The Creative Pre-review API is designed to streamline the ad creation process by offering a preliminary review of your ad creatives. This proactive approach allows you to assess and refine creatives ahead of the standard ad approval process, enhancing the overall quality and effectiveness of submissions. By utilizing this API, you can increase your ad approval rates, ensuring that your campaigns can launch smoothly and without delay.

The benefits of the Creative Pre-review API include:
- **Proactive optimization**: Tackle potential approval roadblocks by addressing concerns during the pre-review, giving your creatives a head start on the journey to approval.
- **Improved approval success**: Creatives that undergo the pre-review process are often better aligned with platform guidelines, resulting in higher approval success rates.
- **Streamlined Processes**: Save time and resources by minimizing back-and-forth revisions, allowing for a quicker turnaround in your ad campaigns.

# Prerequisites

- You've gained access to TikTok API for Business. See [Get Started - Step by step workflow](slug:get-started-guide) for details.
  - To obtain pre-review results for your creatives, you need relevant permissions. See [API Reference](slug:api-reference) to find out permissions required for endpoints (including the endpoints listed in the "Steps" section) and see [Update app permissions](slug:app-permissions) to find out how to configure permissions.

# Steps

To obtain pre-review results for your creatives, follow the following steps:

1. **Create a pre-review task for your creatives directly** using [/creative/pre_review/task/create/](slug:create-a-creative-pre-review-task).
  - Optionally, you can also **create a pre-review task when you upload your video or image to your ad account** using [/file/video/ad/upload/](slug:upload-a-video) or [/file/image/ad/upload/](slug:upload-an-image-reference).

2. **Check the result of the pre-review task** using [/creative/pre_review/task/get/](slug:get-the-result-of-a-creative-pre-review-task).
