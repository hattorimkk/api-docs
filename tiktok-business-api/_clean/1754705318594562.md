# Assets
> Đường dẫn: Marketing API > Business Center > Guides > Assets
You can use Business Center API to manage assets or Asset Groups in a Business Center. 
- [Assets](doc:1754705407307777): Assets include ad accounts, catalogs, pixels, leads, TikTok accounts and stores. 
- [Asset Groups](doc:1754705410074626): An Asset Group is a collection of assets in the Business Center.
