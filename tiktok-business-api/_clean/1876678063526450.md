# How to connect ChatGPT to TikTok for Business MCP Server
> Đường dẫn: TikTok for Business MCP Server > How to connect a ready-made agent to TikTok for Business MCP Server > How to connect ChatGPT to TikTok for Business MCP Server
If you are using ChatGPT, you can connect your ChatGPT account directly to the TikTok Ads platform through the TikTok for Business Model Context Protocol (MCP) Server.

Imagine being able to launch hundreds of ad campaigns in just a few minutes, or scaling up your top-performing ads with a single request. You can also ensure that your ad creatives and product catalogs are autonomously optimized around the clock. The TikTok for Business MCP Server transforms these everyday marketing workflows into native capabilities for your AI agents. This provides your team with a robust, first-class foundation to build and scale your operations on TikTok Ads.

This guide walks you through the connection process, from initial configuration to your first successful interaction. Simply connect, authorize your account, and provide instructions so that ChatGPT can execute advertising operations based entirely on your everyday language.

## Before you start
Before you begin, ensure:
- **You have a TikTok for Business account.** This account is required to authorize TikTok for Business MCP Server so ChatGPT can act on your behalf within the TikTok for Business ecosystem. If you do not have an account, [create a TikTok for Business account](slug:create-a-tiktok-for-business-account) before proceeding.
- You have a ChatGPT Pro, Plus, Business, Enterprise, and Education accounts on the web.

## Step-by-step connection process
### Step 1: Add the MCP Server
a. Log in to your [ChatGPT](https://chatgpt.com/) account. Click on your profile icon in the bottom left corner, and then select **Settings**.

![Select Settings](https://images.ctfassets.net/5jqet0ab9qci/5fiYjfbsWpFuBB6mwIRkbP/8889905dcd48affba950a9dcac8a511f/GPT1.png)

b. Navigate to the **Security and login** tab, and toggle on **Developer mode**.

![Navigate to the Security and login tab, and toggle on Developer mode.](https://images.ctfassets.net/5jqet0ab9qci/5sU4Qv6316juDWPeWfe8Br/6c269e54e031b708f01800dbaa34a58c/GPT2.png)

c. Go to the **Plugins** tab and click the plus (+) button located at the top right of the page.

![Go to the Plugins tab and click the plus (+) button located at the top right of the page.](https://images.ctfassets.net/5jqet0ab9qci/1XQMS4JeLEnfNEnKMJoL4E/5b79556e30ece172c6fcdd37b679837d/GPT3.png)

d. Enter a custom name for your server and the server URL. Then, select the **I understand and want to continue** checkbox, and click **Create**.

![Enter a custom name for your server and the server URL. Then, select the I understand and want to continue checkbox, and click Create. ](https://images.ctfassets.net/5jqet0ab9qci/7L7TSpRODqNzNrvRwnUr3/e64ec472e8b4179b44e0db77ad1d5cde/GPT4.png)

TikTok for Business MCP Server provides two different URLs, so select the one that best matches your use case.

| Server Type | URL | When to use |
|---|---|---|
| Full Disclosure | `https://business-api.tiktok.com/open_mcp/tt-ads-mcp-flat` | All around 400 tools load at connection time. Use it when your AI platform has a large context window and can handle the full tool library without performance degradation. ⏎ Explore the full tool library in [Available tools in TikTok for Business MCP Server](slug:available-tools-in-tiktok-for-business-mcp-server). |
| Progressive Disclosure | `https://business-api.tiktok.com/open_mcp/tt-ads-mcp-layer` | Only around 40 core tools load initially; additional tools are discoverable on-demand. Use it for token-conscious setups or agents with limited context windows. |

e. Click **Sign in with TikTok for Business MCP**.

![Click Sign in with TikTok for Business MCP.](https://images.ctfassets.net/5jqet0ab9qci/1uA4wZ29ktWlzLT7i8sduF/aba9bd96cf9eb5a40068b334dc0ab1ce/GPT5.png)

### Step 2: Authorize the connection
When your agent first connects to TikTok for Business MCP Server, you are prompted to authorize the connection via your TikTok for Business account.

a. If you are not already signed in to TikTok Ads Manager, you are asked to sign in first.

b. Review the permissions requested.

c. Click **Authorize** to grant your AI agent access to your TikTok for Business resources. After authorization, your agent can make tool calls on your behalf.

> **Note:** 
> Authorization lifespan: User authorization for the TikTok for Business MCP Server connection remains valid for a **30-day window**. After 30 days, you will need to reauthorize the connection using the same TikTok for Business account.

![Click Authorize to grant your AI agent access to your TikTok for Business resources.](https://images.ctfassets.net/5jqet0ab9qci/5cy9GSpNSqLMuT95mh8mJz/16934bd2c90de1e64a5d6da41be7bbae/GPT6.png)

![Your agent is now connected to the MCP.](https://images.ctfassets.net/5jqet0ab9qci/5HiLXpZseq1paXReOMk0Si/a950677d38bdb858c52ab817f75ddea9/GPT7.png)

### Step 3: Interact with your AI agent
Your agent is now connected to TikTok for Business MCP Server. Start a conversation with your agent and reference TikTok Ads operations in your prompts. The agent discovers and calls the appropriate MCP tools to fulfill your requests.

> **Note:** 
> Be aware of concurrency limits when managing Upgraded Smart+ Ads. Specifically, if you are using the `smart_plus_ad_create`, `smart_plus_ad_update`, or `smart_plus_ad_status_update` tools, restrict your creation or update calls for a single Upgraded Smart+ Ad to no more than one operation per 5 seconds. Failing to throttle these requests might trigger a concurrency error.

**Example prompts to get started:**
- "Create a new Upgraded Smart+ Campaign for my latest product."
- "Show me the performance report for all active campaigns this week."
- "Upload this E-commerce catalog and check for any diagnostic issues."
