# Upload and publish Skills
> Đường dẫn: Skill and MCP management > Get started with Skill management > Upload and publish Skills
This article covers the complete flow from uploading a new Skill to making it live on Agentic Hub.

## Navigate to My Agentic Hub
All Skill management tasks begin from the **My Agentic Hub** page. To access it:

1. Navigate to the [TikTok for Business Developers portal](https://business-api.tiktok.com/portal) and click **Log in** in the top-right corner.

![Click Log in in the top-right corner](https://images.ctfassets.net/5jqet0ab9qci/2LdMpEwuCM0s9mSv7SoKtY/755c2e351c4eaf9c9685cd682d2c68a4/My_skills_EN-1.png)

2. Click **My Agentic Hub** in the top-right navigation to open the Skill management page.

![Click My Agentic Hub](https://images.ctfassets.net/5jqet0ab9qci/5WDc5fqbBKfUN8tAI4YeQO/9c1e46c1248390c856b0e33c618d41cc/Click_My_Agentic_Hub.png)

> **Note:** 
> All procedures in this article assume you are logged in and viewing the **My Agentic Hub** page. These navigation steps are not repeated in subsequent sections.

## Upload a new Skill
Upload your AI Skill to centralize management, streamline reviews, and distribute your solutions to advertisers through TikTok for Business Agentic Hub.

**Steps**

1. On the My Agentic Hub page, click **Upload New** in the top-left corner and select **Upload skill**.

![click Upload New](https://images.ctfassets.net/5jqet0ab9qci/3AkknZRsFCb6SD5Kc8LzPT/408ffa122910900064cf2f5057115a0a/click_Upload_New.png)

![select Upload skill](https://images.ctfassets.net/5jqet0ab9qci/6752UOKeAlzwgeUXlC8Sie/7e66efe50a08556248029d86dcc7204f/select_Upload_skill.png)

2. Complete the upload form with the following required fields:

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| Skill Name | A unique, descriptive name for your Skill. This field is permanent and cannot be changed after upload. |
| Detailed Description | A clear explanation of what your Skill does, its target use cases, and the value it provides to advertisers. |
| Version | The semantic version number for this release (for example, 1.0.0). |
| Skill File Upload | Your prepared .zip Skill file. See [Prepare an eligible Skill file](https://business-api.tiktok.com/portal/docs/get-started-for-skills-management/v1.3#item-link-Prepare%20an%20eligible%20Skill%20file) for format requirements. |
| Skill Category | The functional category that best describes your Skill. |
| Industry | The industry vertical your Skill is designed to serve. |
| Showcase prompts | Example prompts that demonstrate how advertisers can invoke your Skill. |
| Developer Contact Email | An email address where TikTok can reach you for support inquiries. |
| Legal Disclaimer / Privacy Policy | The links to your legal disclaimer and privacy policy documents. |
| Does this skill require API key or MCP from a third-party service? | A declaration of whether your Skill depends on external third-party API keys or MCP servers. |
| Provide the testing account information or API key | (Required if you answered "Yes" to the previous third-party dependency question) ⏎ Test credentials or API keys that allow TikTok administrators to evaluate your Skill during the review process. Your credentials are securely encrypted and will never be shown on public pages. |
| Does the MCP/API require any paid subscription to the service? | (Required if you answered "Yes" to the previous third-party dependency question) ⏎ A declaration of whether the required third-party API or MCP server involves a paid subscription tier. |
| Countries | The markets where your Skill should be available. |

> **Important:** 
> Choose your Skill Name carefully. Once submitted, this field cannot be modified. If you need to change the name later, you must delete the Skill and create a new one.
> If you provide a video demo for your Skill through the **Screencast Video** field, make sure it includes an English voiceover or English subtitles. Non-English recordings will not be accepted.

![Upload a new Skill ](https://images.ctfassets.net/5jqet0ab9qci/5eUYaz7NqaHLQeTj3fXbXi/5b66c80059561797c848cb6fc1ff4908/Upload_a_new_Skill__4_.png)

3. Select your publishing preference:
- **Immediate publication:** Toggle on **Publish to Agentic Hub**. After approval, your Skill will be published to the marketplace automatically.
- **Deferred publication:** Toggle off **Publish to Agentic Hub**. Your Skill will be reviewed but not published until you manually trigger publication. See the "**Publish an approved Skill**" section below.
4. Review the legal terms and conditions carefully, then check the agreement box.

5. Click **Create Skill** to finalize your submission.

> **Note:** 
> There is no limit on the number of Skills you can upload. Repeat this process for each new Skill you want to add.

## Understand the review process
After submission, your Skill enters the review pipeline. The TikTok team evaluates your Skill for quality, compliance with marketplace policies, and functional completeness. Reviews typically complete within **five to seven business days**.

Monitor progress from the **My Agentic Hub** page by checking the **Status** column:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Pending Review | Your Skill is in the review queue and awaiting evaluation by the TikTok team. |
| Approved | Your Skill has passed review and is eligible for publication. See the "Publish an approved Skill" section below. |
| Rejected | The review identified issues that must be resolved. View feedback and resubmit. See [Resubmit a rejected Skill](https://business-api.tiktok.com/portal/docs/troubleshooting-skills-management/v1.3#item-link-Resubmit%20a%20rejected%20Skill). |
| Published | Your Skill is live on Agentic Hub and visible to advertisers. |
| Unpublished | Your Skill is hidden from discovery. Existing installs display an "unavailable" status. See [Control visibility on Agentic Hub](https://business-api.tiktok.com/portal/docs/manage-published-skills/v1.3#item-link-Control%20visibility%20on%20Agentic%20Hub). |

![Check the Status column](https://images.ctfassets.net/5jqet0ab9qci/RCUWmi1JGEgchoQVmLn2y/2677fa1eaca3e402dd5799435b3a409d/Check_the_Status_column.png)

## Publish an approved Skill
Once your Skill passes review, its status changes to **Approved** — indicating it meets all quality and compliance standards. If you toggled off **Publish to Agentic Hub** during upload, your Skill remains in a ready-but-unpublished state until you explicitly trigger publication.

Publishing makes your Skill visible to all eligible advertisers on TikTok for Business Agentic Hub.

**Steps**

1. On the My Agentic Hub page, locate the Skill displaying an **Approved** status and click **Manage**.

![locate the Skill displaying an Approved status and click Manage](https://images.ctfassets.net/5jqet0ab9qci/4lhUzS4IUEs39kBAiE11dl/980cb3f00a370e1b1c36750163f2a6d2/locate_the_Skill_displaying_an_Approved_status_and_click_Manage.png)

2. On the Skill detail page, click **Publish Skill**.

![click Publish Skill](https://images.ctfassets.net/5jqet0ab9qci/1EfoKoaPdeUYtiI3qtktLF/049aa5ee06366cb9778b737f4318ac4d/click_Publish_Skill.png)

3. Review the confirmation dialog and click **Confirm** to finalize.

![click Confirm to finalize](https://images.ctfassets.net/5jqet0ab9qci/1TSimO7VjMAmSFul6oarUC/dab24c9ded1e18ba654f2f08a393e779/click_Confirm_to_finalize.png)

4. Your Skill is now **Published** — it is live on Agentic Hub and visible to advertisers.
