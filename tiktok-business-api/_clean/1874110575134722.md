# Creative Pre-review
> Đường dẫn: API Reference > Creative Pre-review
The Creative Pre-review API is designed to streamline the ad creation process by offering a preliminary review of your ad creatives. This proactive approach allows you to assess and refine creatives ahead of the standard ad approval process, enhancing the overall quality and effectiveness of submissions.

To learn more about the benefits of and step-by-step instructions for Creative Pre-review API, see [Obtain pre-review results for your creatives](slug:obtain-pre-review-results-for-your-creatives).

This group consists of the following endpoints:

- [Create a creative pre-review task](slug:create-a-creative-pre-review-task)
- [Get the result of a creative pre-review task](slug:get-the-result-of-a-creative-pre-review-task)
- [Upload a video](slug:upload-a-video)
- [Upload an image](slug:upload-an-image-reference)
