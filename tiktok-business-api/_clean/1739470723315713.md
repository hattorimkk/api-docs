# Reach & Frequency
> Đường dẫn: Marketing API > Campaign Management > Guides > Campaign > Reach & Frequency
Reach & Frequency (R&F) is a campaign buying type that helps TikTok For Business advertisers plan and buy campaigns in advance.

To find more details about R&F ads, see [About Reach & Frequency](doc:1744571201117186).
