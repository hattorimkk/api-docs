# Manage published Skills
> Đường dẫn: Skill and MCP management > Get started with Skill management > Manage published Skills
Once your Skill is live on Agentic Hub, you can iterate on it, adjust its metadata, control its marketplace visibility, or remove it entirely. This section covers all ongoing management tasks.

## Upload a new version
As your AI capabilities evolve, keep your published Skills up to date by uploading new versions. Version updates go through the standard review process, but your existing published version remains live throughout, ensuring zero downtime for advertisers currently using your Skill.

**Steps**

1. On the My Agentic Hub page, locate the Skill with a **Published** status and click **Manage**.

![locate the Skill with a Published status and click Manage](https://images.ctfassets.net/5jqet0ab9qci/158Vaq49AgjkYpgBhywaBp/f4b058e46749b569ac39381f345066f1/locate_the_Skill_with_a_Published_status_and_click_Manage.png)

2. On the Skill detail page, click **Upload New Version**.

![click Upload New Version](https://images.ctfassets.net/5jqet0ab9qci/5h4qeTu6tjGtZjE39dwGsi/4cca1c4be1b38ddfec915f11072d7a4b/click_Upload_New_Version.png)

3. Complete the upload form with the required fields:
- **Detailed Description**: update to reflect changes in the new version.
- **Version:** must be strictly higher than the current published version (for example, if the current published version is 1.0.0, use 1.1.0 or higher).
- **Skill File Upload**: your updated Skill file.
- **Skill Category**
- **Industry**
- **Showcase prompts**
- **Developer Contact Email**
- **Legal Disclaimer / Privacy Policy**
- **Does this skill require API key or MCP from a third-party service?**
- **Provide the testing account information or API key** (Required if you answered "Yes" to the previous third-party dependency question) 
- **Does the MCP/API require any paid subscription to the service?** (Required if you answered "Yes" to the previous third-party dependency question) 
- **Countries**

> **Important:** 
> **Skill Name** cannot be changed.
> If you provide a video demo for your Skill through the **Screencast Video** field, make sure it includes an English voiceover or English subtitles. Non-English recordings will not be accepted.

![Complete the upload form with the required fields](https://images.ctfassets.net/5jqet0ab9qci/2WDy9N3vYINX4kPKfzCOxF/bbda29404281f2e03703d5a4610304dd/Upload_a_new_version_EN_3.png)

4. Review and accept the terms and conditions.

5. Click **Submit Version** to finalize.

![Click Submit Version to finalize](https://images.ctfassets.net/5jqet0ab9qci/1rWqHlSGpeOQRH0wjb1Z1s/53fa834e3e6f3a6f669721dfaa171764/Click_Submit_Version_to_finalize.png)

**Version review outcomes**

After submission, the new version enters the review queue (typically **five to seven business days**). Once the review is complete, you can review the result from the Skill detail page.
- **Approved:** The new version replaces the previous version.
- **Rejected:** Your currently published version remains live and unaffected. The new version shows a **Rejected** status on the Skill detail page with reviewer feedback. See [Handle a rejected version update](https://business-api.tiktok.com/portal/docs/troubleshooting-skills-management/v1.3#item-link-Handle%20a%20rejected%20version%20update) for recovery steps.

![The new version shows a Rejected status](https://images.ctfassets.net/5jqet0ab9qci/4zeTbjaLM4Sx80ZX0XqOmz/28a98ae40af561a3d6a807e040075934/The_new_version_shows_a_Rejected_status.png)

## Edit Skill metadata
Edit your Skill's metadata to keep it accurate and well-categorized without initiating a new review cycle. Metadata edits take effect immediately upon confirmation, ensuring your Skill's listing remains up to date with minimal effort.

**Editable fields**

| Field | When to update |
|---|---|
| Detailed Description | Refine messaging, add new feature highlights, or improve clarity based on advertiser feedback. |
| Skill Category | Reclassify your Skill if its primary function has shifted or if a more appropriate category becomes available. |
| Industry | Adjust industry targeting as you expand or narrow your Skill's focus. |

> **Note:** 
> You can edit a Skill regardless of its current status (Pending Review, Approved, Rejected, Published, or Unpublished). To modify other fields, such as the Skill file, version, or Skill Name, upload a new version or delete and re-create the Skill.

**Steps**

1. On the My Agentic Hub page, locate the Skill you want to edit and click **Manage**.

![locate the Skill you want to edit and click Manage](https://images.ctfassets.net/5jqet0ab9qci/jHKOet4hGJmi6U5zb1955/0bf9c8e40afddb70b7e095a249535d38/locate_the_Skill_you_want_to_edit_and_click_Manage.png)

2. On the Skill detail page, click **Edit**.

![click Edit](https://images.ctfassets.net/5jqet0ab9qci/2ABtjmAaN5v7ewDH8UTgK6/883175f6f1459507834bb9beb0e14972/click_Edit.png)

3. Update the desired fields (Detailed Description, Skill Category, and Industry), then click **Confirm**.

![click Confirm](https://images.ctfassets.net/5jqet0ab9qci/6qdA1KFTXdycHy3kpalgxV/352aa03d41914c8c04e3cdf551b26f91/click_Confirm.png)

4. Your changes are saved and take effect immediately. No additional review is required.

![Your changes are saved and take effect immediately](https://images.ctfassets.net/5jqet0ab9qci/306wcdFWxPeKsULiBQ5owi/f04795918270059046303a614f0f8b9d/Your_changes_are_saved_and_take_effect_immediately.png)

## Control visibility on Agentic Hub
Manage your Skill's marketplace presence with the visibility toggle. This feature lets you temporarily hide a Skill from the Agentic Hub marketplace without deleting it, preserving your configuration, review history, and installed user base for when you are ready to re-list.

**When to use the visibility toggle**
- **Scheduled maintenance:** Hide your Skill while deploying breaking changes, then re-enable once stable.
- **Seasonal campaigns:** Temporarily remove industry-specific Skills outside of peak periods.
- **Compliance holds:** Quickly pull a Skill from discovery while resolving regulatory or policy questions.
- **Pre-launch staging:** Toggle off visibility for an approved Skill you are not yet ready to promote publicly.

**Visibility states**

| State | Discovery behavior | Existing installs |
|---|---|---|
| Available (on) | Appears on Discovery and More Skills pages. Advertisers can find and access it. | Fully functional. |
| Hidden (off) | Removed from all discovery surfaces. Not searchable by new advertisers. | Display an "unpublished" status. Functionality may be limited. |

**Steps**

1. On the My Agentic Hub page, locate a Skill with an **Approved** or **Published** status and click **Manage**.

![locate a Skill with an Approved or Published status and click Manage](https://images.ctfassets.net/5jqet0ab9qci/6PuBnn1Pc9lq4m70ivnKuN/75b3eae3d4daed6128d0bd35dc464ccb/locate_a_Skill_with_an_Approved_or_Published_status_and_click_Manage.png)

2. On the Skill detail page, adjust the **Toggle Visibility** setting:
- Set to **on** to make the Skill visible and accessible on Agentic Hub.
- Set to **off** to hide the Skill from all discovery pages.

![adjust the Toggle Visibility setting](https://images.ctfassets.net/5jqet0ab9qci/3lm2Cyqn6WF1GNQmNT2UnN/98639bfefa659bf18b093caddf664fe9/adjust_the_Toggle_Visibility_setting.png)

3. Verify the updated state on the My Agentic Hub page: The **Visibility** column displays **Available** (visible) or **Hidden** (not visible).

> **Note:** 
> Visibility changes are immediate and do not require review. You can toggle visibility as often as needed.

## Delete a Skill
Delete a Skill permanently when you need to retire it entirely. Common reasons for deletion include discontinuing a product, rebranding under a new Skill Name, or removing a prototype that is no longer needed.

> **Important:** 
> Deletion is irreversible. Once deleted: • The Skill is permanently removed from your account. • Advertisers who installed the Skill lose access immediately. • You cannot recover or restore the deleted Skill. If you want to temporarily remove your Skill from the marketplace without deleting it, use the visibility toggle instead. See the preceding **"Control visibility on Agentic Hub"** section.

**Steps**

1. On the My Agentic Hub page, locate the Skill you want to delete and click **Manage**.

![locate the Skill you want to delete and click Manage](https://images.ctfassets.net/5jqet0ab9qci/6xf8mCDWviarg01vhSESzC/3eecda3de73f97fbb37b17971ed35744/locate_the_Skill_you_want_to_delete_and_click_Manage.png)

2. On the Skill detail page, click **Delete Skill**.

![click Delete Skill](https://images.ctfassets.net/5jqet0ab9qci/6jB5clp3IaAKTn1CWGdko3/855912dce576be8cc290eb626f277231/click_Delete_Skill.png)

3. In the confirmation dialog, click **Confirm** to permanently delete the Skill.

![click Confirm to permanently delete the Skill](https://images.ctfassets.net/5jqet0ab9qci/4PZEXrT40SSXMV2AFhWgC2/3ee252811795bf2ad0f1b9d28933d464/click_Confirm_to_permanently_delete_the_Skill.png)

4. The Skill is permanently removed and no longer appears on the My Agentic Hub page.
